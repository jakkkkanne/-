Seigaiha青海波

"Blue ocean waves" — a wish for peaceful, ongoing good fortune · Difficulty ★★☆
— — Stitch line (≈3–4 mm stitch)Template is actual size: 17.8 × 17.8 cm
1. Transfer — Place the template under light fabric (or use carbon paper) and trace the lines with a water-erasable pen.
2. Stitch order — Start with the outermost arcs of the top row, working left to right. Inner arcs come last.
3. Rhythm — Load 3–5 stitches on the needle before pulling through. Keep the top stitch slightly longer than the gap.
4. Finish — Leave a little slack at each turn so the fabric doesn't pucker, then remove the pen marks with water.

Hitomezashi Jūjizashi十字刺し

"One-stitch" cross pattern — stitched on a grid, perfect first project · Difficulty ★☆☆
Grid: 1 square = 5 mmRed = horizontal stitches · Navy = vertical stitches
1. Grid — Mark a 5 mm grid on the fabric (a ruler and fabric pen, or our printable grid on page 12).
2. Horizontal — Work each row left to right: one stitch (1 square long) centred on every other grid point.
3. Vertical — Now stitch vertically through the same points, crossing over each horizontal stitch — the crosses appear!
4. Use it — Coasters, tote bag patches, visible mending on jeans, or framed as textile art.
